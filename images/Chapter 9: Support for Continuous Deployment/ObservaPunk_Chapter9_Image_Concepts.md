### Design Consistency Guidelines
- **Color priority:** Green = healthy/validated, Amber = active/warning/velocity, Red = critical/halt, Cyan = AI/automation/trace, Gold = financial/value
- **Typography on holographic displays:** ALL-CAPS futuristic sans-serif
- **Human figures:** Business and engineering professional attire — this is an enterprise executive audience
- **Background treatment:** Bokeh or controlled depth — no distracting sharp backgrounds
- **Glass panels:** Translucent, edge-lit, frosted — consistent with established ObservaPunk format
- **Data quoted directly from manuscript:** DORA metrics, $14K/min downtime cost, 8 tools/org, 17% compute overhead, 27% cloud waste, 125% ROI, $4.88M breach cost, 55% platform adoption, 7% EU AI Act penalty exposure

### Chapter 9 vs Chapter 8 — Differentiation in Tone
Chapter 8 was about **troubleshooting and resolution** — war room intensity, incident response, the heat of the moment. Chapter 9 is about **deployment velocity with governance** — the pipeline as strategy, the CEO and CFO as stakeholders, the quantum future as design constraint. The imagery should feel slightly more **forward-looking and architectural** where Chapter 8 felt reactive and diagnostic. More blueprint energy, less battlefield.
